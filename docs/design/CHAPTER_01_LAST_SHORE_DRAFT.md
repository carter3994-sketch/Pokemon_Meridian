## Emotional arc
Desperation at sea → first glimpse of an impossibly beautiful but unsettling homeland → battered survival at Landing Camp → first fragile human/Pokémon partnerships → realization that this land is inhabited and not theirs to name → signs of a larger ancient threat.

## Dialogue direction (not final script)
- **Rival:** Clever, impatient, defensive about fear; occasional reluctant acts of care, no relentless insults.
- **Liaison:** Calm under pressure but morally conflicted; short, precise and guarded.
- **Research staff:** Evidence-oriented and exhausted; substantive resource constraints rather than comic relief.
- **Kael:** Has his own reasons and knowledge; direct and wary without a villainous caricature. He calls the place Aevor.

## Continuity and worldbuilding constraints
- No player battle actions before character creation.
- Johto famine and expedition scarcity have immediate stakes, not gratuitous tragedy.
- Native peoples and local Pokémon are not unclaimed resources waiting to be seized.
- Three approved starter species have real ties to Aevor's ancient dragon lineage; their acquisition must be narratively plausible and approved.
- Ship damage does not erase the approved large expedition vessel visible in Landing Camp design.
- Camp setup timing must fit the approved map without requiring Work to alter its composition.
