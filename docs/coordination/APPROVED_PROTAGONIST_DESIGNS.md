## Approved character designs
- Both **male and female** playable protagonists, approximately **18–19 years old**, with minor customization.
- Overall theme: **reluctant explorer / young scientific researcher**, grounded in a poor research family, practical and approachable. Anime proportions and rendering match the previously approved Pokémon-movie-inspired rival and father concept sheet.
- **Male base:** tousled dark-brown spiky hair; cream-and-navy high-collar expedition jacket with gold-and-blue compass patch; dark undershirt; charcoal practical cargo trousers, expedition boots, brown straps and inherited leather field satchel. Recognizably different from wealthy rival's tailored, prestigious navy-and-gold outfit.
- **Female base:** brown hair worn in a high ponytail with loose framing locks; same cream/navy expedition design and compass patch; dark cargo trousers and rugged boots; inherited field satchel; distinctive **red scarf**.
- Both use practical expedition clothing, not wealthy uniforms. Preserve approved silhouette, colors, accessories and clothing distribution.

## Expressive portrait set — mandatory for **each** protagonist
1. **Neutral / resting**
2. **Happy / smiling**
3. **Sad / grieving** (essential for the father's death scene)
4. **Surprised / shocked or scared**
5. **Angry / determined**

The **five emotion portraits for BOTH character options** are explicitly approved and should be the authoritative model for future portrait/sprite work. They are not optional additions.

## Minor customization — approved concept
- Male example variations: slightly longer hair; olive/neutral outfit palette option.
- Female example variations: hair worn down; blue/neutral scarf and outfit accent option.
- Small customization options should not fundamentally alter the approved base designs. Keep overworld and portrait appearance consistent after customization.
- Exact selectable values and GBA implementation constraints require technical planning; do not silently eliminate the core male/female choices or five emotional portraits.

## Important props and story continuity
- Compass expedition patch (gold on blue).
- Field satchel/inherited practical expedition gear.
- **Father's Research Journal** is a separate key item obtained in the approved Medical Facility scene; the character art's visual journal detail is illustrative and must not imply the player already possesses the journal at character creation.
- The protagonist's father dies in the storm, and his legacy is carried throughout the game.
