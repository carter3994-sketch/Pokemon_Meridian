# Pokémon: Meridian — Creative Decisions
**Owner:** ChatGPT Project (creative). **Status:** Established canon; this document records earlier owner decisions.  
**Updated:** 2026-11-10. **Change type:** documentation only, no new owner approvals claimed.

## Established project canon
- GBA fan game using the repository's actual Emerald expansion engine; traditional turn-based Pokémon gameplay.
- Setting about 100 years after Gold/Silver: Johto has collapsed, with food scarcity and desperate refugees.
- Indigenous inhabitants call the new land **Aevor**; the refugee expedition calls it **Meridian**.
- Tone: mature, beautiful, ominous, morally complex, and survival-focused. No reductive presentation of either native or refugee populations.
- Exploration: semi-open areas with major narrative/gyms in a fixed story progression and recurring camp/center relationship scenes.
- Character creation precedes the opening voyage and expedition arrival.
- The protagonist and initially hotheaded/arrogant rival both come from economically struggling scientific/professor families; they gain trust and eventually friendship.
- **Kael** is a young native companion who accompanies the outsiders while maintaining ties to Aevor's people and guardians.
- An expedition government liaison is a decent individual compromised by morally questionable choices.
- Guardians/Wardens distrust outsiders; a rival's capture and release of a guardian is an important story conflict.
- An ancient legendary dragon and a major moral choice about humanity's survival/reset anchor the endgame.
- Starters are three new species families, all dragon-secondary: **Bramblett → Thornval → Sylvaro** (Grass/Dragon), **Pyrrak → Ignivar → Cinderaeth** (Fire/Dragon), **Nereed → Marinys → Thaloris** (Water/Dragon).
- Starter artwork was approved previously. The original approved sprite/art sheets are canonical; names and descriptions alone are **not** authorization to redraw or reinterpret those sprites. Water middle stage is **Marinys**, not Aqualith.
- First evolution at level 16; final evolutions gated by story events (two evolve together, third at later revelation).
- Pokémon from multiple generations; limited new species rather than indiscriminate fakemon; area-specific new catches tied to gym arcs. Do not add Mega Evolution or Z-Moves.
- The evil faction seeks to stop the new-region expedition and unleashes a potentially region-destroying demon Pokémon.
- Landing Camp's **previously approved artwork** sets visual identity: blue/gold expedition markings, custom HQ/lab/medical/supply buildings, living tents, ship/harbor, multi-layer forest, coastal cliffs/waterfall, north ruins/forest, east cave and south route. Its artwork must be inspected before production implementation.

## Chapter 1 — owner-approved premise, not full script
- Title used in the creative draft: **The Last Shore**.
- The expedition **arrives desperate and chaotic**, with a damaged ship, scarce food/supplies, and an emergency shore landing.
- Character creation takes place **before** the voyage/arrival.
- The previous Chapter 1 dialogue, exact food counts, scene sequence, Kael's first lines, how starters were obtained, and chapter endpoint are **proposals pending owner review**, not established canon.

## Approval process
New names, exact dialogue, NPC roles, level pacing, maps and scene transitions must be explicitly reviewed by the owner before being promoted from drafts. Approved files must be labeled **APPROVED** with evidence/date. Engineering may implement generic reusable systems without assuming draft decisions are final.
