# Pokémon: Meridian — Approved Opening Family Legacy

**Status:** APPROVED story direction by project owner, 2026-11-10.  
**Maintainer:** ChatGPT Project (creative direction).  
**Scope:** Father-son emotional foundation and mentor relationship for Chapter 1 and future narrative. Not a complete approved Chapter 1 event script.

## Canon: protagonist's father
- The protagonist's family is poor and part of the scientific research team travelling on the later expedition to Aevor.
- The protagonist's **father** is a humble, hopeful, deeply principled scientist. His work was important enough to earn the family passage despite their financial circumstances. He values knowledge, compassion and cooperation, and believes his research can give ordinary people a future.
- On the ship, he speaks with the protagonist after the expedition briefing, before they sleep and before the storm. He shares his hope that humanity can make a new home and stresses: **“We weren't chosen because we had money. We were chosen because our work mattered.”**
- This is the protagonist's **last conversation with their father**.
- Overnight, a violent storm strikes, the protagonist is knocked unconscious, and **the father dies in the crash**. His death should not be shown gratuitously or directly; the player discovers it after waking in the established Expedition Medical Facility.
- The recurring emotional motif is that **“your father's work mattered.”** People who knew the father recall and honor his contributions throughout the game. They can use varied, natural lines rather than repeating a fixed formula. Over time the phrase shifts from consolation to a meaningful legacy and responsibility.
- Possible later revelation that his research is connected to Aevor's ancient mystery is a **proposal**, not an approved plot fact.

## Canon: rival's father and surrogate-parent arc
- The rival comes from a **wealthy and politically influential family**, in contrast to the protagonist. His father oversees important relations between Johto's settlers and Aevor's native population.
- The rival's father is **intelligent, charismatic, composed, welcoming, and publicly respected**. He sincerely wants humanity to survive but believes harsh compromises can sometimes be justified for the expedition.
- After the storm, he comes to the Medical Facility and **personally tells the protagonist that their father died**. His compassion is genuine; he honors the dead scientist's work.
- He gradually acts as a **trusted surrogate father/mentor**: providing support and a place in the community, checking on recovery, introducing Expedition HQ, guiding the player, and offering the chance to select from the three native starter Pokémon.
- The mentor is not written as an obvious villain at the start. Later moral tension may place his pragmatic choices at odds with the deceased father's values; the player's trust should develop naturally before that conflict.
- A future choice between protecting the protagonist and protecting his expedition is a **proposed character payoff**, not yet a mandated scene.

## Approved context for Chapter 1
- The first expedition had arrived years earlier, established native relations and constructed functioning headquarters and cities. No building a temporary camp during the playable opening.
- The later expedition carries the player and research family. A shipboard briefing covers their mission, dwindling food, and approaching storms. During the nighttime storm, the protagonist is knocked unconscious.
- The protagonist wakes in the established Medical Facility; rival's father later shows them the functional headquarters and reveals three native starter Pokémon: Bramblett, Pyrrak, Nereed.
- A full Chapter 1 rewrite is being developed **collaboratively with the owner**; this document approves these relationship/story foundations, not the entire sequence or finalized scripts.

## Dialogue tone / approved reference beat
The story treatment proposed a final paternal conversation about leaving Johto, the worth of their work, and hope. The mentor's initial bedside exchange is sincere, measured and mournful, including a line like **“Your father's work mattered. And I intend to make sure it wasn't for nothing.”**

The owner confirmed the version matched their intent. These lines are preserved as **approved tonal reference**, but exact script formatting, branching, pacing, scene transitions and speaker text must be reviewed in a dedicated final script pass before production.
