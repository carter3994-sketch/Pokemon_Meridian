## Approved landing camp direction
- Functional modern but modest expedition prefab cabins, solar panels, radio/satellite equipment, medical tent, supply depot, research lab, docks and ship.
- Organic dirt roads, wild surrounding terrain; paved infrastructure comes later in developed settlements.
- Vibrant natural beauty contrasted with eerie ancient structures, restricted cave and an escalating sense of danger beyond the camp.
- Refugee settlements may be visibly under construction; at least one central hub city fully developed.
- All eight gym leaders retained in fixed milestone progression.
- Established native Aevor communities must have distinct histories and built environments from new refugee settlements.
- This is **concept approval only**, not approval of exact route geometry, NPC placements, collision maps, tile assets or final in-engine appearance.
- Previous version 1.0 fantasy/tent-heavy concept was superseded by version 2.0.
