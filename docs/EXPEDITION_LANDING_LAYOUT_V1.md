# Expedition Landing Camp — playable layout proposal v1.0

**Approval status: PENDING.** This is a concept/layout proposal, not an approved tilemap or implemented map. Visual direction v2.0 is approved separately.

## Playable structure
- Start: arrival at the dock on the southern/western waterfront; introductions and controlled first walk into camp.
- Central commons: campfire plaza and dirt-road crossroads with visible entrances and multiple NPCs.
- Expedition HQ: central north-facing building; first mission and expedition leadership.
- West: medical center and research lab, each with explorable interiors.
- East: supply depot, living quarters, and early decisions tied to supplies and trust.
- Northwest high ground: ancient ruins, initially restricted and later accessible through story or choice.
- Northeast: active construction site and optional cliffside cave; construction should signal refugee settlement growth.
- Southeast: optional scenic coastal path with exploration rewards.
- Main forest route: clear marked dirt-road exit; the precise compass direction and transitions must be confirmed on a tile-grid map.
- Northern trail: later-access shortcut / story-gated area.

## Navigation and choice design
- Primary traversal forms a readable loop among dock, commons, HQ, lab/medical, depot and living quarters, with small optional side paths.
- No mandatory progression behind missable criminal penalties; restrictions have designed alternate routes.
- Initial dialogue and object interactions foreshadow shortages, uneasy native relations, and the mysteries of Aevor.
- Story gates, collision, warp coordinates, camera boundaries and NPC placement require a subsequent tile-grid engineering pass.
- Concept art may contain inconsistent compass arrows or exaggerated verticality; exact topology is **not** final until user approves a readable grid plan.
